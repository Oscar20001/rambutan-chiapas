A letter from the orchard

To everyone who buys fruit by the ton:

We grow rambutan in Tuxtla Chico, in the Soconusco region of Chiapas, a few minutes from Tapachula and in sight of the Tacaná volcano. The heat and rain here do what no other place in Mexico can: most of the country's rambutan is harvested in this region.

We cut the clusters by hand, early in the morning, while the fruit is firm. The same day we sort it by size and pack it, so every box that reaches your warehouse is uniform.

We know distance is the challenge. That is why we ship in refrigerated trucks and tell you from the start how many kilometers, how many hours and in which vehicle your order travels.

If you buy for a wholesale market, a retail chain, a produce store or for processing, write to us. We will send you this week's price and what fruit is available.

How long does rambutan last after harvest?

About two weeks refrigerated at 10 to 12 °C with good humidity. At room temperature the skin darkens in three to five days, which is why it always travels in a reefer.

Do you ship to my city?

We ship to any city in Mexico with road access: Monterrey, Saltillo, Chihuahua, Hermosillo, Tijuana, Guadalajara, Mexico City and more. Use the shipping slip to see the distance and time.

What is the minimum order?

One metric ton per order. For weekly volumes through the season we plan a harvest program and set fruit aside for you.

How is it packed?

In 10 kg boxes of fruit of the same size. If your customers need another pack, tell us which one and we will quote it.

Can I get a sample?

Yes. Before your first large order we can send a sample box by courier so you can check size, color and flavor.

Do you sell to the United States or Canada?

Write to us with your city and port of entry. We will go over the phytosanitary requirements and export paperwork with you, and quote freight to the border or to your warehouse.

Can I talk to you before ordering?

Yes. Use the booking calendar to pick a day and time for a phone call, a WhatsApp chat or a video call, Monday to Sunday from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. We speak Spanish and English.

How do I pay?

We agree on it when quoting: usually a deposit to reserve the harvest and the balance when you receive the fruit.
