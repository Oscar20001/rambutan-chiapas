Privacy notice

Ixchel, Frutas del Soconusco, based in Tuxtla Chico, Chiapas, Mexico, is responsible for the personal data you share on this website. This notice explains what we use, why, and how to exercise your rights under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

What we use

Only what you type into the quote form or the booking calendar: name, company, delivery city, phone or WhatsApp, the amount and type of fruit you are interested in, and the day and time you choose for us to contact you. We do not ask for sensitive or banking information on this site.

Why we use it

To answer your quote, call or message you at the time you booked, follow up on your order and send you season updates if you ask for them. We do not sell it or use it for anything else.

Where it is kept

Quote messages travel through WhatsApp when you tap send. Bookings are stored in our own Google calendar and spreadsheet, which only we can access. WhatsApp (Meta) and Google handle that data as service providers, under their own privacy policies. We do not share it with anyone else unless an authority requires it by law.

Your rights

At any time you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Message us on WhatsApp at +52 962 269 9611 or +52 962 170 7553 with your name and request. We reply within 20 business days.

Cookies

This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser only remembers, during your visit, that you already saw the loading animation.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will publish the new version on this page.
